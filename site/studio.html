Studio, the workshop behind every kit

My private app that takes a kit from idea to finished files, sales page and launch plan. Every kit is made in it.

Studio is private: it is how the kits are made, not an app you can sign up for.

The seven steps

What happens at each step, and what comes out of it.

No.StepWhat happensWhat comes out
  1. 01Product idea

    Check the idea against real search and video signals.

    What comes out: A scored idea, locked before any writing
  2. 02Pain to solution map

    List what goes wrong for the buyer and what fixes each problem.

    What comes out: A map of problems and fixes
  3. 03Product structure

    Plan every section and every file the buyer gets.

    What comes out: The list of files the kit promises
  4. 04Draft and design

    Write the content and build the files.

    What comes out: The kit’s files, ready to print or use
  5. 05Offer and sales page

    Choose the tiers and the guarantee, then write the sales page.

    What comes out: Core and Complete tiers, the kit’s own page
  6. 06Launch kit

    Write the launch posts, emails and ads.

    What comes out: A launch calendar
  7. 07Measure and iterate

    Track sales and buyer comments, then plan the next version.

    What comes out: Notes for version 2

Checks before a kit ships

The app holds a kit back until these are true.

  • No missing files

    A kit cannot promise a file that does not exist: the export stops until the file is made.

  • Only real proof

    The app does not write reviews, numbers or guarantees. Proof is only what I type in.

  • Letter and A4

    Every printable file is built in both page sizes.
