Privacy

Short version: this website does not collect anything about you.

Updated 29.Oct.2026

On this website

No cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts. The fonts are served from this site, so no request goes to a font service.

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which handles the basic connection data any website host needs to deliver a page.

When you write to me

The message forms do not send anything themselves. They open your own email app with the message ready; it is sent only if you press send there, to digitiska@protonmail.com. I use your email only to reply.

When you buy a kit

Each kit is sold on its own page. Payment is handled there by the payment provider, not by this website.

The free Solat Times app

Solat Times (solat.digitiska.com) has no account and keeps its settings on your phone. To show your times it contacts: JAKIM e-Solat (only your zone code), OpenStreetMap Nominatim (your position rounded to about 1 km, to find your district) and islamcan.com (the azan sound, only when it plays). If you tap the option to estimate your location, your internet address goes to a location lookup service. The app lists the same in its Settings.
